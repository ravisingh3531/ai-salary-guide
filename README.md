## [SALARY DATA RULES — READ BEFORE WRITING ANY NUMBER]

These rules override everything else on this page. They apply to body copy, tables, quiz results, FAQs, CTAs, image text and schema.

1. **Every salary figure is a role-based indicative range** carrying [VERIFY: source, month/year]. No single-point figures, no topper anecdotes as norms.

2. **Attribute salaries to roles and markets, never to courses.** Correct: "GenAI engineer roles at product companies and GCCs typically span ₹X–Y LPA [VERIFY]." Wrong: "LogicMojo/Scaler graduates earn ₹X."

3. **Acceptable source types:** large-sample aggregators (AmbitionBox, Glassdoor, Naukri JobSpeak, LinkedIn salary data), credible industry reports (NASSCOM, staffing-firm salary guides, Analytics India Magazine studies), and provider-published outcome reports read alongside their stated methodology. 4. **Ranges are median-oriented.** State CTC vs in-hand and fixed vs variable once, early, and link the explanation wherever bands appear.

5. **Always include the honesty counterweights:** entry-level competition, role-title inflation, city and company-type variance, negotiation and prior-experience effects, and the fact that outcomes concentrate among learners who finish and build portfolios.

6. **No salary guarantee, projection or promise anywhere** — including quiz result text, CTA copy, image overlays and structured data. Consistent with ASCI's advertising code as applied to Indian EdTech: no exaggerated outcome or placement claims, no unverifiable statistics.
